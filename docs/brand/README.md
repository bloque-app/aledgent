# aledgent brand

aledgent's marks: `symbol.svg`, one-colour versions (`symbol-mono-ink.svg`, `symbol-mono-white.svg`),
horizontal and stacked lockups for light and dark grounds, and favicons (`favicon.svg` switches to
lilac with a white stop in dark mode; `favicon-16.svg`, `favicon-32.svg`). Self-contained SVG, with the
wordmark converted to outlines.

- **Colour:** aledgent violet `#6a3cff`; the stop is ink `#0d0c17` on light grounds, white on dark.
- **Clear space:** one module (a block plus a gap of the grid) around the symbol, two around a lockup.
- **Minimum sizes:** the full symbol from 24 px; `favicon-32.svg` from 17 to 23 px; `favicon-16.svg`
  at 16 px and below. Horizontal lockup at least 24 px tall, stacked at least 72 px.
- **Don't** recolour, stretch, or put the mark in a tile (except as an app icon).

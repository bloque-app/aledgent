aledgent is a ledger of record: identities own **ledger accounts**, value moves between them in one
**book**, and value enters and leaves through one **settlement layer**. It is reached through one HTTP
service, **ingress**. Every route needs a bearer credential, except the two under `/v1/auth`, which check
what their body carries. Amounts are decimal-integer strings of minor units, identifiers are
`did:aledgent:` strings, and times are milliseconds since the Unix epoch.

## Before your first call

Nothing creates the first principal from nothing. The deployment that runs aledgent gives you:

- **an identity** for your service, opened for you by its identities principal;
- **an API key** for it, whose secret you generated and whose SHA-256 you sent: see
  [API keys from zero](../../integrate/api-keys/);
- **the grants** you will act under on your users' accounts, if you act on accounts you do not own: see
  [Grants](../../integrate/grants/);
- **a notification destination and secret**, registered for your principal: see
  [Notifications](../../integrate/notifications/).

[Principals and credentials](../../integrate/principals-and-credentials/) and
[Which key do I call with?](../../integrate/which-key/) explain who is calling and what it may touch.

## One transfer, from request to decision

<Steps>

1. **Send a transfer**, with your API key and a client key of your own:

   ```sh
   curl -X POST "$INGRESS/v1/transfers" \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY" \
     -H "client-key: payout-2026-10-13-0042" \
     -H "content-type: application/json" \
     -d '{"legs":[{"from":"did:aledgent:acct:…","to":"did:aledgent:acct:…","asset":"TSTA","amount":"125000"}],
          "reference":{"invoice":"inv-8812"}}'
   ```

   The client key makes a retry the same operation. Derive it from your own ids, never from a timestamp.

2. **Read the acknowledgement.** By default a transfer is acknowledged at `Received`, with `202`:

   ```json
   {
     "operation_reference": "did:aledgent:op:…",
     "state": "Received",
     "class": "open-non-refunding",
     "terminal": false,
     "client_key": "payout-2026-10-13-0042",
     "client_key_derived": false,
     "reference": { "invoice": "inv-8812" }
   }
   ```

   This is an acknowledgement, not an outcome: nothing is decided yet and no balance has changed.
   `reference` is your own pass-through data, echoed back; it is not the operation reference.

3. **Learn the decision by notification**: `Settled`, or `Rejected` with its condition named. Use
   `GET /v1/operations/{reference}` only to recover or confirm one. A `503 ERR-DecisionUnavailable` or a
   network error is retried with the **same** client key: see [While the core is away](../../integrate/core-away/).

4. **Read the book**: balances, movements and statements. See [Reading the book](../reading-the-book/).

</Steps>

Cites `NFR-11`, `REQ-OP-8`, `REQ-OP-6`, `REQ-CS-7`, `REQ-SA-7`, `REQ-IN-5`, `REQ-EV-6`, `REQ-PR-1`,
`REQ-PR-5`, `REQ-PR-8`, `NFR-9`, `REQ-SN-2`, `REQ-PV-5`, `REQ-OS-1`, `F-017`, `F-018`.

Six things must be continuously observable (`NFR-11`): the age of the oldest non-terminal operation,
observation completeness, the count and age of undelivered notifications, anchor lag, the reconciliation
outcome per axis, and the liquidity of every position that funds settlement-layer costs. Every signal is a
count, an age or a time, never a ledger fact: no account, customer amount, identity or operation reference
appears in one.

## Where they are read

| Source | What | Who may read it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /v1/ops/signals` | The core's signals, one consistent view, ages in milliseconds at the time of the read | A principal holding a live `read_signals` operator grant. The read is attributed like any other (`REQ-PV-5`) |
| The settlement adapter's signals file | Observation, positions and fee lanes: only the adapter reads the settlement layer | The host's own processes |
| The reconciliation's run records, the backups' manifests, the last drill's record | Reconciliation outcome per axis, backup ages, drill result | The host's own processes |
| `opsctl exporter` | Joins all of the above into Prometheus metrics on its own `/metrics`, on every scrape | Your Prometheus |
| `GET /metrics` on ingress | That ingress instance's own metrics | Your Prometheus |

The exporter reads the core through ingress, as a principal that should hold `read_signals` and nothing else. Its
configuration names ingress, the token's file (or the variable that holds it, never an argument), the
adapter's signals file and the records directories; `ops/exporter.example.toml` in the repository is a
starting point. A source that does not answer is itself a signal: `aledgent_signals_up{source=…} 0` pages
(`AledgentSignalsDown`), and an adapter signals file older than a minute pages
(`AledgentAdapterSignalsStale`).

The alert rules are `ops/prometheus/rules/aledgent.yml` and the dashboard is
`ops/grafana/dashboards/aledgent-operations.json`, both in the repository. **Thresholds are the
deployment's**: the values below are the shipped defaults. Every rule labels its alert with its `signal`
and an `owner` (shipped as generic team names), which you map to your own teams in Alertmanager. Severity `page` pages a human;
`warning` shows on the dashboard only.

## Age of the oldest non-terminal operation

`REQ-OP-8`. How long the oldest operation that has not reached `Settled`, `Released` or `Rejected` has been
open. A large age means something is waiting on evidence, on a human, or on a core that is not deciding.

| Read as | Metric |
| --- | --- |
| `oldest_open_age`, with `open`, `received` and `needs_review` counts | `aledgent_oldest_non_terminal_age_ms` |
| `oldest_undecided_age`: the oldest command ingress holds that no core has decided yet (`REQ-CS-7`) | `aledgent_oldest_undecided_row_age_ms` |
| `review`, `review_overdue`, `review_most_overdue`, `review_service_level`: the `Needs-Review` queue (`REQ-OP-6`) | `aledgent_review_queue`, `aledgent_needs_review`, `aledgent_review_overdue`, `aledgent_review_most_overdue_ms`, `aledgent_review_service_level_ms` |

| Alert | Fires when | Runbook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AledgentOldestNonTerminalOverServiceLevel` | Oldest non-terminal operation over 1 h, for 5 min | [restore](../runbooks/restore/) |
| `AledgentReceivedOverDecisionBound` | A received command undecided for over 60 s, for 1 min | [restore](../runbooks/restore/); for a restarting core, [Core restarts](../core-restarts/) |
| `AledgentReviewOverdue` | Any `Needs-Review` operation past its service level | Resolve it: [Overview](../overview/#when-an-operation-needs-a-human) |

## Observation completeness

`REQ-SA-7`, `REQ-IN-5`. Whether the book has seen everything the settlement layer recorded for this
deployment. The adapter never advances past a range it did not read; a range it was forced to skip is
recorded as an explicit gap.

| Metric | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `aledgent_observation_cursor_lag` | Inbox positions the adapter's cursor is behind the finalized inbox |
| `aledgent_observation_gaps_total` | Ranges the adapter was ever forced to skip |
| `aledgent_observation_chain_mismatches` | Entries read that do not lead to the head the layer holds, after a second read |
| `aledgent_observation_stopped` | Observation waiting for an operator: a range no reader can serve, or the layer reset under a running adapter |

| Alert | Fires when | Runbook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AledgentObservationGap` | Any skipped range | [gap filling](../runbooks/gap-filling/) |
| `AledgentSettlementChainMismatch` | Any chain mismatch | [gap filling](../runbooks/gap-filling/) |
| `AledgentObservationStopped` | Observation waits for an operator | [gap filling](../runbooks/gap-filling/) |
| `AledgentSettlementEpochRefused` | The core refused to follow a new sequence of the layer's record; new withdrawals are refused meanwhile | [gap filling](../runbooks/gap-filling/) |
| `AledgentObservationLag` | Cursor more than 100 positions behind, for 10 min | [gap filling](../runbooks/gap-filling/) |

## Undelivered notifications

`REQ-EV-6`. How many notifications are owed and not yet acknowledged by their recipient, and the age of the
oldest. A notification whose principal has no registered destination or secret waits here; so does one that
used up its delivery attempts. See [Notifications](../../integrate/notifications/).

| Read as | Metric |
| --- | --- |
| `undelivered`, `oldest_undelivered_age`; each one, with its recipient and age, at `GET /v1/notifications/undelivered` | `aledgent_undelivered_notifications`, `aledgent_oldest_undelivered_age_ms` |

| Alert | Fires when | Runbook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AledgentUndeliveredNotification` | A notification owed for over 15 min, for 5 min | None yet: check the recipient's registration and that its endpoint answers `2xx` |

## Anchor lag

`REQ-PR-1`, `REQ-PR-8`, `F-017`. The time since the newest checkpoint whose anchor is final on the
settlement layer was created; before the first is final, the age of the oldest checkpoint. Anchoring is
hourly by default and one checkpoint at a time. With no new posting the cadence makes no checkpoint, so a
quiet book's lag grows without being late: what matters is a large lag **while postings wait** past the
latest checkpoint, which means anchors are not reaching finality. A lag reduces provability only: holders' operations go on
([While the settlement layer is away](../../concepts/settlement-layer-away/)).

| Read as | Metric |
| --- | --- |
| `anchor_lag`, `anchor_in_flight_age`; every checkpoint with its status at `GET /v1/ops/anchors` | `aledgent_anchor_lag_ms`, `aledgent_anchor_in_flight_age_ms` |
| `anchor_unanchored_entries`: postings after the latest checkpoint's entry sequence (every posting, before the first checkpoint). Absent from a core that does not report it, which then exports no series | `aledgent_anchor_unanchored_entries` |
| `anchor_incidents`, per kind | `aledgent_anchor_incidents{kind=…}` |

| Alert | Fires when | Runbook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AledgentAnchorLag` | Newest final checkpoint older than 2 h **and** postings waiting past the latest checkpoint, for 10 min. It cannot fire against a core that does not report the waiting postings | [anchoring](../runbooks/anchoring/) |
| `AledgentAnchorStuck` | A checkpoint in flight for over 1 h | [anchoring](../runbooks/anchoring/) |
| `AledgentAnchorCommitmentMismatch` | The layer recorded an anchor of this number with another commitment; anchoring stops | [anchoring](../runbooks/anchoring/) |
| `AledgentUnknownAnchor` | The layer holds an anchor aledgent never made | [anchoring](../runbooks/anchoring/) |

## Reconciliation outcome per axis

`NFR-9`, `REQ-PR-5`. Reconciliation runs at least daily, at one minor unit and zero tolerance, on three
axes: (1) each (account, asset) against every independent source; (2) the per-asset trial balance, exactly
zero, and the in-transit position against the credits in transit; (3) the book against the settlement
layer's view of what aledgent holds. A failure pages a human; it never opens a ticket. `opsctl reconcile`
runs it and writes the records the exporter reads.

| Read as | Metric |
| --- | --- |
| Per axis: differences found, whether it could run, time since its last run | `aledgent_reconciliation_differences{axis=…}`, `aledgent_reconciliation_ran{axis=…}`, `aledgent_reconciliation_last_run_age_ms{axis=…}` |
| `settlement_differing`, `settlement_comparison_age`: the latest settlement comparison | `aledgent_settlement_differing_assets`, `aledgent_settlement_comparison_age_ms` |
| `Suspense` balances not at zero, and the oldest | `aledgent_suspense_nonzero`, `aledgent_suspense_oldest_age_ms` |

| Alert | Fires when | Runbook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AledgentReconciliationDifference` | Any difference on any axis | [reconciliation failure](../runbooks/reconciliation-failure/) |
| `AledgentReconciliationNotRun` | An axis has not run in 24 h | [reconciliation failure](../runbooks/reconciliation-failure/) |
| `AledgentSettlementDifference` | The latest settlement comparison differs, by as little as one minor unit | [reconciliation failure](../runbooks/reconciliation-failure/) |
| `AledgentSuspenseNotReconciled` | A `Suspense` balance not zero for over a day | [reconciliation failure](../runbooks/reconciliation-failure/) |

## Liquidity of the positions that fund settlement

`REQ-SN-2`. aledgent bears every settlement-layer cost, so the positions that pay for it must not run dry
unseen. A shortfall is a named, parked, self-clearing condition: the affected withdrawals park, one alert is
raised per position, and they resume on their own when it clears.

| Metric | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `aledgent_position{asset=…}` | The deployment's position per asset on the settlement layer, in the layer's units |
| `aledgent_lane_native{lane=…}` | A signing lane's native balance, which pays the fees |
| `aledgent_lane_under_fee_floor{lane=…}` | Whether that lane is under the fee floor |
| `aledgent_liquidity_alerts{kind=…}` | Open shortfalls, one per position: `kind` is `position` or `fees` |

| Alert | Fires when | Runbook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AledgentLiquidityShortfall` | Any open shortfall; withdrawals are parked | [crank outage](../runbooks/crank-outage/) |
| `AledgentLaneUnderFeeFloor` | A lane under its fee floor | Fund the lane |

## Beyond the six

The same rule file pages on more than `NFR-11` asks: completion over its 30 s ceiling at p99
(`AledgentCompletionOverCeiling`), a ticker, which moves deadlines, silent for over 90 s
(`AledgentTickerStale`; an idle book ticks about once a minute), the
settlement layer's finalized height not moving (`AledgentBlockProductionStalled`), a fault answered
`ERR-Internal`, an audit gap, late backups, a failed or overdue restore drill, and the core's halt
(`AledgentCoreHalted`). Each names its runbook in the rule's `runbook` annotation.

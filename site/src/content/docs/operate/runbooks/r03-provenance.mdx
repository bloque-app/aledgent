<Aside type="note" title="Status in this release">
No message crosses Agents with **one core Agent**, the single-voter profile this release deploys. The metrics
come with the sharded profile's host; the alert rules are committed so that they page the day the metrics
appear.
</Aside>

## What it is for, and when to run it

Run it when `AledgentR03CheckFailed` pages (at once), or `AledgentR03Unverified` (a message unverified for over a
minute, for five minutes). Suggested owner: security, with platform.

Every message between the book's Agents (`credit/<n>`, the principals `feed`, and `trim/<n>`, a peer telling a
sender how far it applied its credits) is checked by the receiving Agent, in its own transition, against its
sender's certified commit: a hash chain over the stream, and the sender's voter's signature over the chain's
head. Each Agent has one voter, whose key is held by the signer.

In **`report`** mode, the default, the check runs but does not gate (`enforce` refuses a failing message): a
message that fails is **applied anyway**. So a page means that a message its sender did not commit has probably
already moved value. Treat it as a security incident, never as noise.

Each failure writes one line to the core host's standard error:

```json
{"event":"r03_check_failed","mode":"report","receiver":"book-1","stream":"credit/0","reason":"forged","applied":12,"verified":10,"applied_anyway":true}
```

| `reason` | What it means |
|---|---|
| `forged` | The messages applied do not chain to the sender's certified head: one was written, changed, reordered or left out after the sender committed it. The relay or its transport. On `trim/<n>`: a trim past what the peer certified, or a peer that applied credits this shard never sent; in `report` the credits were forgotten anyway, and only the relay's copy still delivers them |
| `wrong_committee`, `bad_signature`, `non_canonical`, `insufficient_quorum`, `unknown_sender`, `wrong_agent` | The certificate is not the pinned voter's, for that sender. A forged certificate, or pins that drifted from the deployment |
| `wrong_program` | The sender's commit ran under another runtime program than the one pinned: an unannounced runtime change |
| `not_in_claim` | The certificate carried with the stream does not cover it: a relay fault |

A stream that failed **keeps failing**: once a forged message is applied, the receiver's chain no longer meets
its sender's. Nothing clears it yet; an operator's re-base after an incident is later work.

## Preconditions

- The receiver, the stream and the reason, from the log line.
- Whether one stream fails or many: one points at a sender or one link; many point at the relay.
- For `AledgentR03Unverified`: whether certificates stopped (the sender's host cannot reach the signer) or the
  relay stopped carrying them.

## Steps

1. **Contain.** Stop the relay (it runs as its own process and OS user). Nothing crosses Agents while it is
   stopped. Credits wait in transit: no value is lost, and it is neither payer's nor payee's until the credit
   lands. Revocations wait too: freeze, through the command surface (`freeze_account`,
   `POST /v1/ops/accounts/{account}/freeze`), the accounts any exposed credential reaches until the feed runs
   again (`NFR-20`).
2. **Measure the effect.** Run the reconciliation on demand (`opsctl reconcile`). A forged credit shows on the
   second axis, in transit; a forged `settle` or `evidence` on the third. A forged `feed` entry (a grant) shows
   on no axis: list the grants the receiving shard applied since the stream's last verified sequence (the log
   line's `verified`) and compare them with the principals Agent's registry. Any difference: follow
   [Divergence](../divergence/). Correct only through the command surface: a reversal by two principals. Never
   edit a store.
3. **Find the writer.** For `forged` or `not_in_claim`: the relay's host. Keep its process, memory and logs as
   evidence. For a certificate reason: the signer's log of every use of the voters' keys, and the pins the
   deployment admitted; a signature the signer never made means the key left the signer or its secret store. For
   `wrong_program`: compare the runtime each Agent pins with the release's, and follow
   [Divergence](../divergence/).
4. **Rotate.** The relay's credential always ([Key rotation](../key-rotation/)). A voter's key if any
   certificate reason is involved: a new key means pinning its committee again in every receiver, which is the
   deployment's step through each Agent's driver, never the relay's.
5. **Restart the relay** only when the writer is found and removed.
6. **Write the incident** under your incident process. What never goes in a message: keys, tokens, account
   identifiers.

## Verify

- Reconciliation on demand: every axis at zero, or every difference owned and being corrected through the
  command surface.
- No new `r03_check_failed` line, and `aledgent_r03_unverified_age_ms` back under a minute.
- The failing stream is recorded in the incident with its last verified sequence: it keeps failing until the
  re-base exists.

## Roll back

Nothing here edits a store. Setting the mode to `off` silences the check; it is never a fix. A freeze is lifted
with `POST /v1/ops/accounts/{account}/unfreeze` once the feed runs again.

Cites `REQ-SA-7`, `REQ-SN-2`, `REQ-OP-9`, `F-018`.

## What it is for, and when to run it

Run it when `AledgentBlockProductionStalled`, `AledgentLiquidityShortfall` or `AledgentLaneUnderFeeFloor`
pages. Suggested owners: settlement operations for blocks, treasury for liquidity.

**Rule that does not bend:** nothing is released on a timer and nothing is refunded while the layer is
stalled (`REQ-OP-9`). Parked withdrawals resume by themselves when the shortfall clears (`REQ-SN-2`).

## Blocks stalled

The settlement layer produces blocks only while its coretime is cranked; an idle chain can stop. While the
finalised height does not move, nothing is observed, nothing is final, and deadlines keep running: operations
move to `Needs-Review`, never to a refund.

### Preconditions

- On the dashboard: `aledgent_finalized_height` is flat, `aledgent_finalized_stall_ms` is rising, and the
  adapter is still `serving`.

### Steps

1. Confirm the case with [Finality stall](../finality-stall/).
2. Crank: submit the chain's own block-production trigger from the crank account (the chain operators'
   procedure; its key is not the adapter's). This step has been exercised against a local network only, not
   automatically.
3. Verify the height moves, then that the adapter's cursor catches up
   (`aledgent_observation_cursor_lag` back to zero) and it accepts the layer's head with no chain mismatch
   ([Chain mismatch, gaps and resets](../gap-filling/) if it raises one). A stall longer than a full node
   keeps events (about 256 blocks) needs a node that keeps history.
4. Operations that reached `Needs-Review` while stalled are resolved with the layer's records as evidence
   through the command surface (`resolve_operation`, `POST /v1/ops/operations/{reference}/resolve`), one by
   one. None is released without evidence.

## Liquidity or fees short

The adapter parks an instruction whose position (less what it has signed and not settled) or whose lane's fees
cannot cover it, raises one alert per position, and resumes it when the shortfall clears.

### Steps

1. Read which: `aledgent_liquidity_alerts{kind}`, `aledgent_position{asset}`, `aledgent_lane_native`.
2. Whoever holds the treasury tops up the instance's position, or the lane's native balance, on the layer (the
   pallet's calls from the treasury's own keys).
3. Do not resubmit anything: the adapter signs each operation at most once, ever.

## Verify

- The finalised height moves, the alert clears, and parked withdrawals resume.
- The adapter's cursor lag is back to about zero.

## Roll back

Cranking and top-ups are not reversed. Nothing here changes aledgent's stores; a top-up that was wrong in
amount is corrected on the layer by the treasury, and the book follows what the layer records.

Cites `NFR-19`, `NFR-20`, `REQ-RT-11`, `REQ-RT-13`, `REQ-AU-3`, `REQ-AT-3`, `F-031`, `F-033`.

<Aside type="note" title="Status in this release">
None of these lags exists with **one core Agent**, the single-voter profile this release deploys. A credit is
applied in its debit's commit. A revocation takes effect at its commit; the only lag left is ingress's API-key
cache, whose lifetime is 500 ms and which ingress refuses above the 1 s bound of `NFR-20` at start
(`api_key_cache_ms`). There are no streams between Agents and no native decide. The metrics come with the
sharded profile's host; the alert rules are committed so that they page the day the metrics appear.
</Aside>

## What it is for, and when to run it

Run it when `AledgentCreditLag` (p99 over 2 s), `AledgentRevocationLag` (p99 over 1 s), `AledgentStreamLag` or
`AledgentReplayFenced` pages. Suggested owner: platform; security for revocation and the fence.

## Preconditions

- Which signal: credit lag (a peer shard's `credit/<n>` stream), revocation lag (the principals Agent's `feed`),
  a stream's lag (the relay), or a shard's replay fence.
- Whether one shard or every shard: one points at that shard's host; every shard points at the relay or the
  principals Agent.

## Steps

1. **Credit or stream lag.**
   - Check the relay between hosts: its connections, and the receiving shard's decision rate.
   - A shard behind its own load is a capacity question ([Scale out and in](../scale-out-in/)). Rebalance hot
     accounts with ordinary transfers (`REQ-RT-12`).
   - The value in transit is neither payer's nor payee's until the credit lands: never move it by hand.
2. **Revocation lag.**
   - A revoked credential may act until the revocation reaches every shard. Treat a lag over `NFR-20`'s bound as
     a security matter.
   - Check the principals Agent's `feed` stream and the slowest shard.
   - If an exposed credential is involved, freeze the accounts it reaches through the command surface
     (`POST /v1/ops/accounts/{account}/freeze`) while the feed catches up.
3. **Replay fenced** (native decide).
   - A shard's replay through the runtime disagreed with its native decision, and the shard fenced itself: it
     decides nothing more.
   - This is a divergence: follow [Divergence](../divergence/). Do not unfence by hand.
   - Turn native decide off, then restart the shard from its last committed state.

## Verify

- The p99 lags are under their bounds for 30 minutes.
- Reconciliation on demand: axes 1 and 2 at zero, and every partition's in-transit position balances against
  its peers.

## Roll back

Redeploy the previous digest of the shard host or relay. Nothing here edits a store, and nothing moves value
outside the command surface (`REQ-AT-3`). Lift a freeze with `POST /v1/ops/accounts/{account}/unfreeze`.

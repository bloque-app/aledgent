Cites `REQ-OS-1`, `REQ-AU-5`, `REQ-ID-6`, `REQ-AU-7`, `REQ-AT-1`.

Every operator capability is its own operation kind, with its own authority, audit record and required
justification (`REQ-OS-1`). There is no "all of the above". **The operator holds none by default**: every
operator command needs a live operator grant of that kind, held by the operator's identity.

## The routine

<Steps>

1. **Grant yourself one kind, briefly.** `expires` is milliseconds since the Unix epoch; make it minutes
   away, not days.

   ```sh
   curl -X POST "$INGRESS/v1/ops/operator-grants" -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPERATOR_TOKEN" \
     -H "client-key: og-register-asset-2026-11-20" -H "content-type: application/json" \
     -d '{"principal":"'$OPERATOR'","kinds":["register_asset"],
          "justification":"Register CRD for the credits integration, ticket 123",
          "expires":'$(( $(date +%s) * 1000 + 15 * 60 * 1000 ))'}'
   ```

   `result.grant` is the grant.

2. **Act.** Here, register the asset (see [Registering an asset](../registering-assets/)).

3. **Revoke.**

   ```sh
   curl -X POST "$INGRESS/v1/ops/operator-grants/$GRANT/revoke" -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPERATOR_TOKEN" \
     -H "client-key: og-register-asset-2026-11-20-revoke" -H "content-type: application/json" \
     -d '{"justification":"Done"}'
   ```

</Steps>

Every step is attributed and audited (`REQ-AU-7`, `REQ-AT-1`), with its justification.

## Reading the refusals

| Refusal | Means |
|---|---|
| `ERR-AuthorityRevoked` | A grant of that kind existed and was revoked |
| `ERR-NotAuthorised` | None ever existed, or it lapsed (`expires` passed) |
| `ERR-JustificationRequired` | The command came without a `justification` |

## The kinds

Each is granted on its own: `resolve_operation`, `resume`, `propose_reversal`, `approve_reversal`,
`freeze_account`, `unfreeze_account`, `register_asset`, `deprecate_asset`, `remove_asset`,
`issuance_authority`, `declare_adapter_support`, `privileged_read`, `import_book`,
`load_opening_balances`, `anchor_checkpoint`, `compare_settlement`, `legal_disclosure`, `read_signals`,
`import_credentials`, `open_identity`. The [operator API](../../api/operator/) lists the routes.

Operator authority reaches only accounts in delegated custody, and never, through a grant over all of
them, an account the operator's own identity owns (`REQ-AU-5`).

## `open_identity` is different

`open_identity` is the **identities principal's role** (`REQ-ID-6`). It is granted alone, never combined
with another kind: a grant that mixes it with others is `ERR-NotAuthorised`. At most one principal besides
the operator holds it at a time; granting it to another revokes it from the holder in the same operation.

The operator does not grant it to itself to open identities: the operator opens identities by policy.

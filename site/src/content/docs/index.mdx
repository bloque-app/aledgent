## Four ideas carry the whole system

Read these first. Everything else in the guide refines one of them.

<CardGrid>
	<Concept n="01" label="Book" title="A deterministic kernel">
		Balances live in a deterministic kernel, in exact integer minor units, and every input is kept
		with its reply, so the book's state can be re-derived. A book starts from a verified genesis and
		takes no command until the check passes.
	</Concept>
	<Concept
		n="02"
		label="Operations"
		title="Received, then decided"
		href="/concepts/operation-lifecycle/"
		linkText="The operation lifecycle"
	>
		Every command gets an operation reference. A transfer is acknowledged at `Received`, then
		decided. A client key makes a retry the same operation, never a second one.
	</Concept>
	<Concept n="03" label="Anchoring" title="A commitment on Kreivo">
		Every hour, and on demand, a commitment to the book's state goes to a settlement contract on
		Kreivo. aledgent cannot later claim to have held another state. It says nothing about solvency.
	</Concept>
	<Concept n="04" label="Ingress" title="One authenticated entry">
		Services call with tokens, people with passkeys, and every route is denied by default. Each
		command is checked against the grants its principal holds, for that account, asset and bound.
	</Concept>
</CardGrid>

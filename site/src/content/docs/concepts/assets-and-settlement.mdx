## One registry, one precision

An **asset** is a unit of value with a registered identity and a fixed precision. There is exactly one
asset registry, and an asset's precision is recorded there and nowhere else (`REQ-AS-1`, `INV-24`). It is
never carried by a name, an account or a caller's input, and it never changes.

- **Amounts are exact integers of minor units** (`REQ-AS-4`, `INV-7`). There is one conversion between a
  decimal and minor units, at one boundary, and it never rounds: an amount with more precision than its
  asset is refused with `ERR-PrecisionExceeded`.
- **Asset ids are normalised at every boundary** (`REQ-AS-3`), to uppercase: two spellings of one asset
  cannot exist as two assets.
- **Assets are kept apart** within an account: no operation mixes two assets' balances. aledgent does not
  convert between assets; a conversion is a redemption of one and an issuance of another, each its own
  operation.

The registry is read with `GET /v1/assets`: each asset's id, precision, kind (`fiat`, `stable`, `native`,
`other`), status (`active`, `deprecated`) and binding.

## How value enters and leaves the book

Value crosses aledgent's boundary in only two ways, and both post against the **issuance counterparty**,
the named position whose balance is the negative of all value aledgent holds:

| Way | Who | Posting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Issuance and redemption** | A principal holding issuance authority for the asset, within its bounds (`REQ-AS-6`) | `Issue` (`POST /v1/issuances`) or `Redeem` (`POST /v1/redemptions`), answered with its decision |
| **The settlement layer** | A deposit observed on it, or a withdrawal executed through it | `Issue` at an inbound leg's settlement, `Redeem` at an outbound leg's |

For every asset, the entries across the whole book sum to exactly zero (`INV-5`), so the trial balance makes
every issuance measurable. Internal transfers move value between ledger accounts and never touch the
settlement layer, which holds no record per operation, account or identity (`REQ-SL-5`).

## Book-only, then bound

Every asset is **registered book-only** (`REQ-AS-7`): in the registry, bound to nothing on the settlement
layer. Its value enters and leaves only by issuance and redemption, and moves inside the book like any
other asset's. An external leg in it is refused at submission with `ERR-UnsupportedLeg`.

A book-only asset may be **bound** to the settlement layer once, by a holder of issuance authority for it,
with a justification (`REQ-AS-8`, `POST /v1/ops/assets/{asset}/bind`). The binding names the asset's
location on the settlement layer, and succeeds only if:

- the settlement layer describes a live asset there whose symbol, normalised, is the asset's id and whose
  precision is the registry's, read from the layer's own fields for them, never parsed from a name;
- no other asset is bound, or being bound, to the same location;
- **zero divergence:** what the settlement layer holds for aledgent in the asset equals, to the minor unit,
  the book's outstanding balance of it, established by the settlement layer and final there.

While a binding is in progress the asset is `binding`, and its issuance and redemption are refused, so the
amount being checked cannot move. A refused binding returns it to book-only. Once final, it is `bound`:
external legs in it are supported as the settlement adapter declares, issuance and redemption go on under
issuance authority, and the book is reconciled against the settlement layer's holding from that point. A
binding is never undone, and after it the settlement layer's description of the asset no longer changes the
registry (`REQ-AS-9`).

How an operator registers, binds and, for one registered wrongly and never used, removes an asset
(`REQ-AS-10`): [Registering an asset](../../operate/registering-assets/).

## The settlement layer and its adapter

A deployment has exactly one **settlement layer**: the one external network through which value enters and
leaves aledgent, and on which anchors are published (`INV-25`). The plan binds it to a settlement contract
on Kreivo (`DEC-17`), and `DEC-20` describes how a bound asset maps to a location there. The specification defines it by what it must do:
record a transfer at a coordinate that is unique, stable and independently readable, and offer a point
after which a recorded fact cannot be revised (`REQ-SL-3`).

The **settlement adapter** is the one component that touches it. It holds the keys to execute outbound
legs and observes the layer, and it holds no authority over any ledger account (`REQ-SA-8`):

- it **submits evidence and executes instructions**, and never writes the book or declares an outcome
  (`REQ-AR-2`, `INV-11`). aledgent derives every status from what was observed;
- it **declares what it supports**, for which assets, and aledgent refuses an unsupported operation at
  submission, not at execution (`REQ-SA-1`, `ERR-UnsupportedLeg`);
- at start-up it **asserts it is connected to the declared settlement layer**, and refuses to serve on a
  mismatch (`REQ-SA-2`). While it does not serve, withdrawals are refused with
  `ERR-SettlementLayerUnavailable`;
- it reports each instruction as **executed, provably not executed, or unknown**, and never collapses
  "unknown" into either of the others (`REQ-SA-4`).

aledgent bears every settlement-layer cost. No holder-facing flow shows a fee, a funding step or a fee
asset (`REQ-SN-1`); a shortfall in the positions that pay for settlement parks the affected operations and
raises one alert per position, and they resume when it clears (`REQ-SN-2`).

## A deposit

1. A counterparty sends value on the settlement layer to the deployment, with an **opaque account
   reference** that the deployment resolves to one ledger account (`REQ-SL-7`). The settlement layer
   holds no record of the account itself. An operator prints the reference for an account with
   `aledgent-ingress account-ref <account>`.
2. The adapter observes the arrival at its coordinate. aledgent deduplicates on that coordinate (`REQ-IN-1`)
   and credits whether or not it started anything. The operation enters at `Observed`. **Nothing is
   credited yet** (`INV-10`).
3. Once the layer's finality has passed the observation and the canonical record still matches
   (`REQ-IN-3`), the operation is `Final`, and then `Settled` with one `Issue` posting to the account.
4. If the record no longer matches before finality, the operation is `Orphaned` and released. Nothing was
   posted, so nothing is reversed.

## A withdrawal

1. **Reserve.** `POST /v1/withdrawals` reserves the amount against the payer (`REQ-SO-1`). It is
   acknowledged at `Received`; its decision follows.
2. **Submit.** `POST /v1/withdrawals/{reference}/submit` names the destination. aledgent assembles the
   instruction itself (`REQ-SO-2`), with the operation reference and an explicit expiry (`REQ-SL-4`,
   `REQ-SL-8`), and hands it to the adapter: `Submitted`.
3. **Observe.** The leg's inclusion is observed: `Observed`. The command has completed (`NFR-15`), but
   the value has not left the book.
4. **Finality.** Finality passes the observation: `Final`, then `Settled` with one `Redeem` posting from
   the reservation to the issuance counterparty.
5. **If it cannot execute.** A proved rejection, or proven non-inclusion after the expiry, releases the
   reservation at once. If neither can be proven, the operation goes to `Needs-Review` and stays reserved:
   it is never released on silence ([Reservations](../reservations/)).

An executed amount that differs from the reservation is `ERR-AmountMismatch`, with the difference posted
explicitly, never left as a silent residue.

<Aside type="note">
A withdrawal's command completes at **inclusion** (`Observed`), within `NFR-15`. The time from inclusion
to finality is mostly the settlement layer's, is measured and reported, and is not bounded by aledgent.
</Aside>

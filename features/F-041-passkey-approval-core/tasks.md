# F-041 — tasks

**Blocked.** By `F-040` (Feature A) and by `T-041-00` with the owner's threshold. Only `T-041-07` (the stand-ins) and
`T-041-00` (the measurement) start first, and `T-041-00` itself starts only after the owner has confirmed its threshold
(amendment 0037, ruling 9 and Q-F) and `F-040`'s `T-040-02` has frozen the formats. The owner's ruling `w14`: both
alternatives, (b) a record per command and (c) a principal of its own, are measured before one is chosen. These are the handover's tasks B-00 to B-06 under their numbers here.
Amendment 0037 is applied to `SPEC.md` v0.4.29.
